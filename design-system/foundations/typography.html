Typefaces

Primary display: Montserrat (bundled OTF, 400–800) — titles, headings, eyebrows, kickers, on-bar labels. Body: Open Sans (bundled TTF) — panels, menus, inputs, captions. Monospace only for token values, never UI. Emphasis pattern: mix Light + Bold inside one headline to stress keywords.

Scale — every size in the app

App title / launcher heading15px · Montserrat 700

Body — panels, menus, inputs12px · Open Sans 400
Secondary — meta, hints, toolbar attribution11px · Open Sans 400
Section labels / eyebrows10px · Montserrat 700 · caps · +0.12em
On-bar labels (SVG)10px · Montserrat 700 · phase bars
Badges, pills, chip initials9px · 700 · floor — never smaller
Kickers and eyebrows carry the brand's wide tracking (.12em); everything else stays dense. Sentence case in titles — never Title Case.

The kicker device

Projects
The red kicker rule (28px × 3px) above an uppercase Montserrat label is the brand signature — used for panel headers, launcher sections, and empty-state headings. A thin red vertical bar to the left of a label is the sub-kicker for inline section labels.
